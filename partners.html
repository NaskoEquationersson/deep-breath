Partners

Deep Breath brings together four organisations from Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia, combining experience in social inclusion, education, youth work and grassroots sport. The partnership brings together complementary expertise to support the use of sport and physical activity as tools for inclusion, well-being and rehabilitation.

BUSOLA

Płock, Poland – Project Coordinator

en.busola.edu.plzakladkarnyplock@gmail.com

The Socially Excluded Education Association “Busola” was established in Płock, Poland, in 2016. The organisation works primarily in the fields of education, social inclusion and rehabilitation, with particular experience in supporting prisoners and other people at risk of social exclusion. Its activities include educational, cultural, sporting, health-promoting and training initiatives. Busola also has extensive experience in European cooperation and Erasmus+ projects, particularly in adult education and initiatives supporting disadvantaged groups. Within Deep Breath, Busola acts as the project coordinator, bringing its experience in project management, education and social rehabilitation to the partnership.

NOVA

Çanakkale, Türkiye – Project Partner

www.novader.orgwe@novader.org

NOVA Association for Culture, Arts and Youth was established in Çanakkale, Türkiye, in December 2024. The organisation works in the fields of youth, culture and arts, social inclusion and environmental sustainability, with particular attention to disadvantaged young people. NOVA organises workshops, seminars, campaigns and local and international activities and promotes active participation, democratic values and intercultural dialogue. Operating mainly through volunteers, the association cooperates with local authorities, schools, universities and civil society organisations and contributes experience in non-formal education, dissemination, digital communication and international youth work to the Deep Breath partnership.

TEAM SOLARIS

Struga, North Macedonia – Project Partner

www.teamsolaris.mkkosta1981.kh@gmail.com

Cycling Sport Club Association Team Solaris is a sports association established in Struga, North Macedonia, in 2010 by former athletes. Guided by the principle of “Sport for All”, the organisation began with cycling activities and gradually expanded its work to hiking, orienteering, kayaking and other outdoor sports. Inclusion has become an important part of its activities, with initiatives such as KAYAK4ALL providing opportunities for people regardless of gender, ability or socioeconomic background. Team Solaris also organises multidisciplinary community sports events and has experience in Erasmus+ Sport cooperation, bringing strong grassroots sport and inclusive physical activity expertise to Deep Breath.

BASLSK

İzmir, Türkiye – Project Partner

bucaataturksporlisesisporkulub@gmail.com

Buca Atatürk Spor Lisesi Spor Kulübü (BASLSK) was established in İzmir, Türkiye, in 2023. The club has a team of experienced physical education teachers, coaches and volunteers and works with around 100 athletes. Its activities include gymnastics, football, badminton, bocce and table tennis, as well as water sports, cycling, hiking, camping and other outdoor activities. BASLSK places particular emphasis on increasing the participation of disadvantaged children and young people in sport, including young people with disabilities, refugees, migrants and those with limited economic opportunities. The club contributes its coaching, grassroots sport and inclusive physical activity experience to Deep Breath.
