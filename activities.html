Activities

Deep Breath combines training, sport and physical activity, local cooperation, European initiatives and international exchange. Explore the activities carried out by the project partners throughout the project period.

Deep Breath Program – First Step

January – June 2026. Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

Completed

The first step of the Deep Breath Program focused on strengthening the competences of coaches working with people who may need additional support in their well-being and social inclusion. During the six-month programme, participating coaches took part in theoretical and practical training activities delivered with the support of experts. The programme focused particularly on developing professional, communication and social skills and on preparing coaches to respond sensitively to different needs and individual circumstances.

The knowledge and skills gained during this phase prepare the participating coaches for the second step of the programme, where sport, exercise and social activities are used to support well-being, participation and social inclusion.

Deep Breath Program – Second Step

July 2026 – June 2027. Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

Ongoing

The second step of the Deep Breath Program puts the knowledge and skills developed during the first phase into practice. With the support of participating coaches and experts, project partners are carrying out sport, exercise, social and educational activities designed to promote well-being, active participation and social inclusion.

Activities are adapted to different needs and local contexts, using sport and physical activity to create supportive environments, encourage social interaction and contribute to positive and sustainable changes in participants’ daily lives.

Europe to Local Activities

2026 – 2027. Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

Ongoing

Europe to Local Activities aim to bring European initiatives, values and opportunities closer to local communities through project partners. Throughout the project, partners organise and share activities linked to European initiatives and special EU days, while promoting participation, inclusion and a stronger connection between local communities and the European dimension of sport.

The activities include European Week of Sport events in 2026 and 2027, the quarterly “Our Europe” publications and communication activities related to special European days.

Greener Sports Activities

July – September 2026. Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

Ongoing

Greener Sports Activities support project partners in making their sport and organisational practices more environmentally responsible. During this phase, partners explore practical measures that can reduce environmental impact and encourage more sustainable approaches in their regular activities.

The work also promotes environmental awareness among organisations, coaches and participants and supports the integration of greener practices into local sport activities and future project initiatives.

Local Sustainability Activities

February 2026 – Ongoing. Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia.

Ongoing

Local Sustainability Activities focus on building stronger cooperation between project partners and relevant organisations in their local communities. Through communication, planning, local meetings and preparatory activities, partners work to develop local networks and create a sustainable structure that can continue beyond the project period.

These activities also contribute to the preparation of the Deep Breath Support Centers, which are intended to strengthen local cooperation and help sustain the project’s approach and results after the project ends.

International Project Meetings

2025 – 2027. Poland, North Macedonia and Türkiye.

Ongoing

International project meetings bring together representatives of all Deep Breath partner organisations to coordinate project activities, evaluate progress and exchange experiences. The meetings provide an opportunity to review completed activities, plan the next stages of the project and strengthen cooperation between partners.

Four international meetings are planned throughout the project period in Poland, North Macedonia and Türkiye. Each meeting focuses on the needs and progress of the relevant project phase.
