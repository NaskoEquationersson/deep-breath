About Deep Breath

Deep Breath is an Erasmus+ Sport Small-scale Partnership project that uses sport and physical activity as tools to support individuals recovering from substance addiction. The project aims to promote social inclusion, mental well-being and rehabilitation while strengthening the professional, psychological and communication skills of coaches working with this vulnerable group.

Bringing together four organisations from Poland, Türkiye and North Macedonia, Deep Breath combines expertise in sport, education and social support. The project first provides specialised training for coaches and then involves individuals in recovery in tailored sports and physical activities. To create a lasting impact beyond the project period, Deep Breath Support Centers will also be established in each partner country, providing community-based support through sport.

Project Objectives

  • To equip coaches and sports trainers with the professional and communication skills needed to work effectively with individuals affected by substance addiction.
  • To support recovery and rehabilitation from substance addiction through sport and physical activity and contribute to lasting positive outcomes.
  • To strengthen the capacity of sports clubs and NGOs in addressing addiction and develop cooperation mechanisms at local and European levels.
  • To contribute to a more informed and effective response to substance addiction in partner countries and across European society.

Project Phases

  2. B. Implementation Period

  3. C. Follow-up Period
